Idle Angler is a fishing game for a TRMNL. The angler casts every 15 minutes on its own, day and night, whether or not the device or a browser is on. The screen shows the water in its season, the time of day and weather, the latest catches and the week's heaviest fish at that water. Every day or two the player opens the companion to sell the catch, restock bait and save for the rods and passes that open new waters. A full cooler releases new catches but still logs them, so time away costs no progress. There are no streaks and no benefit from refreshing; two alerts (cooler full, out of bait) are off unless the player turns them on. A weekly code on the screen adds a cosmetic fly to the player's fly box. It is the second game on TRMNL Games, after Desk Crawler, so it shares that account and companion at trmnlgames.com.

Installation: the sign-up and connect flow is the same one you tested and approved for Desk Crawler (plugin 564): same trmnlgames.com account, same one-time code or Google/GitHub sign-in, same Connect this TRMNL installation step. If you already made a TRMNL Games account for that review, it works here too. I haven't recorded a new install video for that reason, but I'm happy to send one if it helps.

Testing: no demo credentials are needed. In TRMNL, install plugin 568 and choose Connect. On trmnlgames.com, sign up with any email address your team owns (one-time code), Google or GitHub, pick a public name and choose Connect this TRMNL installation. Back in TRMNL, Save. The first screen shows a QR code to the companion; the first cast, which always lands a fish, follows within 15 minutes. Configure opens the companion's management page for that installation. Removing the installation keeps the angler.
