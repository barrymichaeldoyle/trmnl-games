The companion needs focused UX improvements, not a visual redesign. The strongest gaps are in Idle Angler; Desk Crawler already supplies useful patterns to reuse.

## Priority improvements

1. **P1 — Preserve the game through sign-in and setup.** Live verified: opening /app/idle-angler shows a Desk Crawler explanation, About link, marketplace installation and Help destination. Make the entry explanation, preview and all destinations game-aware. This is the first fix: someone following an Angler link can install the wrong game. Evidence: apps/web/src/lib/platformShell.tsx:18,25-36 and apps/web/src/lib/platformHeader.tsx:29.
2. **P2 — Keep failed confirmations open for retry.** Source verified: shop purchase, sell-all, mounting and pause handlers close their sheet regardless of useIntent.run returning false. Close only on success; preserve the target and display the error with Retry/Cancel in context. Evidence: apps/web/src/routes/app/idle-angler/shop.tsx:179-183; cooler.tsx:151-155,172-176; settings.tsx:111-115; apps/web/src/lib/intent.ts:53-86.
3. **P2 — Keep cooler selection and selling within reach.** Source verified: the selected-fish sale button and total appear after the entire catch list. A player selecting near the top must scroll to discover and complete the action. Reuse Desk Crawler's sticky selection bar: count, total, clear selection and Sell selected. Evidence: apps/web/src/routes/app/idle-angler/cooler.tsx:101-139; apps/web/src/routes/app/desk-crawler/inventory.tsx:316-323. Mature-account mobile geometry remains unverified.
4. **P2 — Warn before consuming trophy-size fish.** Source verified: bulk sale warns about fishmonger premiums but omits trophy-size specimens; Use as bait is immediate. Add a conditional trophy consequence and a way to exclude those fish. Preserve fast routine clearing; do not add confirmation to every ordinary sale. Evidence: apps/web/src/routes/app/idle-angler/cooler.tsx:108,120,157-162. This is a precaution against losing the specimen, not the recorded personal best.
5. **P2 — Put operational status before optional Dock updates.** Source-supported hypothesis: fly entry and What's new precede scene/status, while the bare-hook warning follows recap. Put a compact paused/full-cooler/no-bait summary close to the name, followed by the scene and optional updates. Keep fly discovery prominent. Validate full-cooler, empty-bait and multiple-callout mobile fixtures before settling the layout. Evidence: apps/web/src/routes/app/idle-angler/index.tsx:96-105,120-153.

## Design specificity and strengths

The pixel worlds, plum shell, gold buttons and real device previews feel authored for these games. Keep them. Shared busy controls retain focus and reserve label width; notices announce outcomes and persistent errors; progressive unlocks avoid overwhelming players with unavailable destinations. The main cognitive-load issue is conditional Dock hierarchy, rather than the five-tab navigation itself.

First-time visitors lose confidence at the wrong-game gate. Returning owners benefit most from clear operational status and quicker cooler clearing. Cautious players need consequences and retry context at the end of a transaction.
